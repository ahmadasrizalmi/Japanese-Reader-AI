## Brand & Style

This design system blends modern Japanese editorial elegance with tactile warmth and playful assistive clarity. Rooted in the visual craft of traditional lacquerware, washi paper, and refined contemporary typography, the system bridges a sophisticated aesthetic (deep maroon crimson and soft off-white canvas) with friendly, delightful interactions.

### Target Audience & Emotional Resonance
The interface is designed for language learners, readers, and bilingual researchers handling the cognitive load of Japanese literacy (Kanji, Kana, and Furigana). The UI creates a serene, welcoming sanctuary—eliminating textbook sterility while preserving academic rigor. It instills confidence, calmness, and tactile gratification through smooth surfaces, pill-shaped tactile touchpoints, and gentle ambient elevations.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Tactile Material 3:** Grounded in Google Material 3 container-first patterns with expressive Japanese craft sensibilities.
- **Wabi-Sabi Warmth:** Replaces harsh cold digital whites and sterile grays with nuanced eggshell tones, warm vermilion, and tea-tinted neutrals.
- **Claymorphic Playfulness:** Incorporates soft, puffy clay-like 3D assets and icons bathed in natural diffuse light, adding an inviting, human touch to rigorous linguistic study.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around warm, comforting contrasts that ease prolonged reading sessions while offering clear visual cues.

### Primary & Brand Colors
- **Primary (`#9E3D3D`) & Crimson Deep (`#802B2B`):** Derived from traditional Japanese lacquer and vermilion ink seals (*hanko*). Used for primary calls-to-action, bottom navigation active states, reading progress anchors, and critical state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53`):** Soft vermilion used for subtle promotional badges, gamified streaks, and active flashcards.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373`):** Warm toasted sand for secondary chips, grammatical particle highlights, and meta accents.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F5` / `#F5EFEB`):** Soft washi-inspired background that avoids the optical glare of raw digital white.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated containers that frame Japanese reading passages, study decks, and grammar breakdown drawers.
- **Text Palette:** Deep charcoal-espresso (`#2D2424`) ensures crisp, legible Kanji strokes without the harshness of pure black, supported by warm stone neutrals (`#6E6262` and `#9C8E8E`) for Furigana and auxiliary metadata.

### Dynamic Reading Pastel Highlights
Interactive reader sentences and tokens utilize an array of tranquil pastels upon selection. These pastels maintain low saturation (luminance > 90%) to guarantee full compliance with foreground charcoal text:
- **Soft Yellow (`#FEF3C7`):** General active sentence focus.
- **Soft Green (`#D1FAE5`):** Recognized vocabulary and JLPT mastery tier.
- **Soft Pink (`#FCE7F3`):** Grammar pattern focus and particle relationships.
- **Soft Blue (`#DBEAFE`):** Foreign loan words (Katakana) and proper nouns.
- **Soft Lavender (`#EDE9FE`):** AI-generated contextual notes and mnemonic hooks.
- **Soft Mint (`#D1F4E0`):** Saved bookmarks and user-flagged sentences.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs contemporary geometric warmth with strict typographical rules for multi-script East Asian rendering.

### Script Pairing & Fallback System
The primary digital face is **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering friendly curved terminals and open counters that match claymorphic iconography. For Japanese characters (Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana), fallbacks resolve cleanly to **Noto Sans JP** and system Japanese engines (`Hiragino Sans`, `Yu Gothic`).

### Japanese Reader Layout & Furigana Spacing
- **Vertical Clearance (`body-reading-jp`):** Japanese reader text mandates an expanded line height (`42px` at `20px` font size). This generous baseline clearance guarantees that native HTML5 `<ruby>` / `<rt>` Furigana glosses sit comfortably above complex Kanji radicals without overlapping previous lines or shifting line-heights dynamically during tap interactions.
- **Ruby Ratio:** Furigana text uses `body-furigana` (`11px`), centered directly over the base token with an opacity of 0.82 to keep primary Kanji legibility dominant.
- **Section Headers (`label-caps`):** Subdued, tracked-out uppercase typography (`0.08em` letter-spacing) anchors section divides such as `READING INTENSITY` and `API KEYS (BYOK)`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first ergonomics following Material 3 guidelines and comfortable single-thumb reading arcs.

### Grid & Canvas Rhythm
- **Scaffold & Margins:** The mobile canvas adheres to an outer boundary margin of `1.25rem` (20px), tapering to `1rem` (16px) on compact handheld viewports (< 360dp).
- **Component Breathing Room:** Modules, flashcards, and analytical metric blocks utilize an 8pt spatial baseline rhythm. Element gaps within card interiors maintain `0.75rem` to `1rem` vertical separation.
- **Reader Safe Zones:** In the reader view, horizontal margins are locked at `1.25rem` to prevent edge distortion, while bottom insets account for an elevated dynamic sheet or persistent contextual action pill (`space-2xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a tactile, soft-ambient depth model. Heavy, aggressive dropshadows are replaced with warm, tinted atmospheric diffusions reminiscent of gentle sunlight filtering through paper screens.

### Ambient Shadow Palette
All elevations feature a soft crimson/terracotta undertone (`rgba(128, 43, 43, 0.04 - 0.08)`) instead of synthetic neutral black, creating a warm, organic presence on off-white backgrounds:
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat, un-elevated warm off-white (`#FAF7F5`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Library Items):** White (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle ambient spread: `0px 6px 20px -2px rgba(158, 61, 61, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Action Bars & Selection Toolbars):** Elevated pill containers with `0px 10px 28px -4px rgba(128, 43, 43, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Contextual Grammar Bottom Sheet):** Smooth sliding modal surface with high-radius corners and a protective dimming scrim (`rgba(45, 36, 36, 0.35)`).

### Tactile 3D Claymorphic Details
Key decorative headers, streak tokens, and category badges incorporate smooth, diffused claymorphic elements. These assets feature dual-direction soft highlights (upper-left soft ambient white rim, lower-right warm terracotta bounce), lending a welcoming, toy-like tactile charm to vocabulary tracking.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-rounded, soft geometric profile (Pill-shaped level 3) to produce an approachable, pressure-free learning experience.

### Radius Assignments
- **Article & Study Cards:** Extra-rounded `24px` (`rounded-3xl`) containers that frame content like smooth ceramic tiles.
- **Buttons & Filter Chips:** Full-pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for touch targets, interactive category selectors, and furigana toggle segments.
- **Interactive Sentence Highlights:** In-line text selections utilize `6px` to `8px` rounded corner spans that smoothly wrap multi-line Japanese phrases without visual breaks.
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets:** Top corners styled with `28px` to `32px` radiuses, emphasizing the sheet's comfortable pull-tab nature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Buttons:** Pill-shaped, clad in Primary Crimson (`#9E3D3D`), with bold white Plus Jakarta Sans text. Subtle internal top highlight adds tactile depth. Hover/pressed states deepen to `#802B2B` with a gentle `scale(0.98)` spring.
- **Secondary / Tertiary Buttons:** Crisp white or warm beige pills with tinted crimson text (`#9E3D3D`) and soft borders (`1.5px solid #F5EFEB`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact pill containers (`rounded-full`) used for JLPT levels (N5–N1), article categories, and reading time tags.
- Inactive state: Pure white background with muted charcoal typography.
- Active state: Saturated primary maroon fill or soft pastel highlight fills (`#FEF3C7`, `#D1FAE5`) with matching deep accent text.

### Interactive Reader Canvas
- **Sentence Tokens:** Clean, transparent backgrounds in default reading state. On tap, instant transition to a randomly assigned dynamic pastel highlight (`#FEF3C7`, `#D1FAE5`, `#FCE7F3`, `#DBEAFE`, `#EDE9FE`, `#D1F4E0`) with smooth rounded bounds.
- **Furigana Toggles:** Segmented pill controls (`On`, `Tap`, `Off`) that smoothly fade phonetic annotations without disturbing vertical layout geometry.
- **Contextual Selection Toolbar:** Floating dark or crimson pill hovering directly above selected text, holding instant action icons for copy, audio pronunciation, and AI grammar inspection.

### Cards
- **Library Article Cards:** Rounded-3xl (`24px`) white containers resting on warm canvas. Features a clean two-column or stacked layout with JLPT difficulty badges, estimated read time, and a soft claymorphic thematic illustration.
- **Grammar & Sentence Analysis Sheet:** Material 3 modal bottom sheet containing pronunciation audio triggers, Kanji-by-Kanji semantic breakdowns, and natural language translations grouped in rounded inner tiles (`#FAF7F5`).

### Form Inputs & Scratchpad
- **Instant Scratchpad / Search:** Generously padded text fields with `18px` to `24px` corner radii, soft warm off-white fill (`#F5EFEB`), and focused boundary borders in primary crimson (`#9E3D3D`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth pill-segment toggles and circular selectors filled with crimson when checked, providing clear visual tactile feedback.
